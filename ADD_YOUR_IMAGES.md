# 📸 How to Add Your Beautiful Tilapia Images

## Quick Steps (5 Minutes)

### 1. Save Your Images
Save the 5 tilapia photos from our conversation to your computer:
- Image 1: Fresh tilapia on ice (multiple fish)
- Image 2: Single tilapia 
- Image 3: Fried tilapia on banana leaf
- Image 4: Tilapia on the grill

### 2. Rename the Images
Rename them exactly as shown:
```

2. single-tilapia.jpg
3. fried-tilapia.jpg
4. tilapia-on-grill.jpg
5. grilled-tilapia-plate.jpg
```

### 3. Copy to Project Folder
Copy all 5 images to this folder:
```
c:\Users\LIFE GOES ON\Desktop\Tilapia-joint\tilapia-website\public\images\gallery\
```

### 4. Refresh Your Website
Go to http://localhost:5175/gallery and refresh the page (Ctrl + F5)

**Your beautiful tilapia images will now appear in the gallery!** ✨

---

## Image Recommendations

For best quality:
- **Format**: JPG or JPEG
- **Size**: 800x800px to 1200x1200px
- **File size**: Under 500KB each
- **Quality**: High resolution, good lighting

---

## What Happens After Adding Images

Your gallery will show:
1. **Fresh Daily Catch** - Tilapia on ice
2. **Quality Selection** - Single tilapia
3. **Perfectly Grilled Tilapia** - On plate
4. **Charcoal Grilling Process** - On grill
5. **Crispy Fried Tilapia** - Golden brown

The remaining 7 gallery items use high-quality stock photos until you add more of your own images.

---

## Need Help?

If images don't appear:
1. Check filenames match exactly (including .jpg extension)
2. Make sure images are in the correct folder
3. Try hard refresh (Ctrl + Shift + R)
4. Check image file sizes (should be under 2MB each)
