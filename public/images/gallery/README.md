# Gallery Images

## How to Add Your Tilapia Images

Please save your tilapia images in this folder with the following filenames:

### Required Images:
1. **fresh-tilapia-ice.jpg** - Fresh tilapia on ice (Image 1 you showed)
2. **single-tilapia.jpg** - Single fresh tilapia (Image 2 you showed)
3. **grilled-tilapia-plate.jpg** - Grilled tilapia on plate (Image 5 you showed)
4. **tilapia-on-grill.jpg** - Tilapia being grilled (Image 4 you showed)
5. **fried-tilapia.jpg** - Fried/cooked tilapia (Image 3 you showed)

### Steps:
1. Save the 5 images from your conversation to your computer
2. Rename them according to the list above
3. Copy them to this folder: `public/images/gallery/`
4. Refresh your website - the images will appear automatically!

### Image Specifications:
- Format: JPG, JPEG, or PNG
- Recommended size: 800x800px to 1200x1200px
- Keep file sizes under 500KB for fast loading

The gallery will automatically display these beautiful tilapia images!
